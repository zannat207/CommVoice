Understand changes in your community, in plain words

When your council or a public body proposes a change, the documents are written for lawyers — not for the people it affects. CommVoice explains them in everyday words, calmly and without jargon, and answers your questions only from what the document actually says. If it isn't in the document, it tells you, instead of guessing.
